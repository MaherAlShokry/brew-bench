Delete your account

The Brew Bench, by Maher Al Shokry

You can delete your Brew Bench account, and everything synced to it, at any time.

In the app or on the website

  1. Tap your picture at the top of the screen (on a computer, at the end of the tab bar) to open your profile.
  2. Tap Edit.
  3. At the bottom, tap Delete my account and profile, then confirm.

If it asks you to sign in again first, sign out and back in, then try once more.

Or ask us

Email thehope99.ms@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Brew Bench account”. We’ll delete it within 30 days and let you know.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your email address and password, or the link to your Google account.
  • Your profile: nickname, name, the few words about you, photo, favourites and city.
  • Everything synced to your account: coffees and diaries, photos, brews, recipes, scans, Top 5, trophies, favourites, gear, calibration and settings.

It is removed straight away and is not kept in backups.

What is not deleted

  • The copy of your data on your own phone or browser. Remove it by deleting the app, or clearing the site’s data in your browser.
  • Brews and recipes you added to a shared log, which belong to the group. Delete them from the shared log first if you want them gone.

See also our privacy policy.
