Why grinders need different numbers

The 1Zpresso grinders use an external ring read as rotation.number.click. Close the burrs until they just touch: that is zero. Turning toward bigger numbers opens the burrs and grinds coarser. Most filter brewing stays inside the first rotation, so the rotation digit is usually dropped: 5.6 means number 5, click 6.

The two dials are not interchangeable. The ZP6 Special has 9 numbers per turn (90 clicks, about 22 microns each); the K-Ultra has 10 (100 clicks, 20 microns each). The same number on both grinders is a different grind size, and different burr shapes mean even the same micron gap tastes different. Calibrate each one by taste.

Why a V60 is 5.6 on one and 7.2 on the other. 56 ZP6 clicks is a gap of about 1.2 mm; 72 K-Ultra clicks is about 1.4 mm. The K-Ultra needs the wider gap because its all-round burrs make more fine particles, which slow the drawdown and pull more out of the coffee. The ZP6's filter burrs make very few fines, so it needs a tighter gap to reach the same flow and strength. The numbers also follow 1Zpresso's own ranges for each grinder (filter roughly 4.5 to 7.0 on the ZP6, 7.0 to 9.5 on the K-Ultra).

Across brands the numbers mean even less: a Comandante counts clicks from closed, an Ode or Encore has a numbered dial, a Niche is stepless. The app places every grinder on one shared scale, anchored on its typical V60 setting, so it can suggest a starting point for any brewer and match a setting from one grinder to another. For grinders you haven't calibrated these are starting points from typical ranges: fine-tune by taste and save them under Calibrate to your grinders on the dial-in.

      The bloom

      The bloom degasses CO2, wets every particle evenly so later pours don't channel, and starts extraction early. Its length is a real variable.

      BloomEffectBest for
      ~30sOften incomplete degassing on fresh beans. Uneven flow, thinner and brighter cup.Older beans, darker roasts, loud anaerobics
      ~45sEnough gas out, even saturation, balanced.Most coffees
      ~60sReal extraction during the bloom. More body and sweetness, some risk of bitterness and temperature drop.Very fresh, light, dense roasts

      The fresher and lighter the roast, the longer the bloom can go.

      Extraction levers

      More extraction

      Finer grind (biggest lever), hotter water, longer contact, more agitation, more water per gram.

      Less extraction

      Coarser grind, cooler water, gentler pours, fewer pulses, faster drawdown.

      Coarse and hot

      Cleaner cup. Fewer fines, less muddiness and astringency, while heat still pulls sweetness. The classic light-roast approach, and a natural fit for the ZP6.

      Fine and cool

      More intensity and acidity from the surface, but fines can still over-extract and turn drying. The K-Ultra's extra body makes this easier to live with.
