## Full description (4000 max)

The Brew Bench is a coffee field guide and dial-in tool for home brewers. Brew better cups, keep every coffee you’ve had, and learn where it all comes from.

DIAL IN EVERY CUP
• Grind settings for your own grinders, with the cup to expect: brighter or sweeter, lighter or fuller
• Recipes from World Brewers Cup and AeroPress champions, scaled to your dose and ratio
• A brew timer that walks you through every pour
• A taste troubleshooter: sour, bitter or flat? It tells you what to change

SCAN THE BAG
• Take a photo of the label: origin, process, variety, roast date and roastery are read on your phone
• A brew plan made for that coffee and your gear

KEEP EVERY COFFEE
• Your bean gallery: photos of the bags, ratings in coffee beans, tasting notes
• The bags you’re drinking now, with how fresh they are and how many cups are left
• A diary for each bag: opening it, the cup that clicked, the one you shared, the last one, with photos
• Your Top 5 of the year, honourable mentions, trophies, and a coffee passport stamped with every origin

EXPLORE COFFEE
• An origins map with regions, harvests and stories
• 53 varieties, from Geisha and Pink Bourbon to Ají and SL28, with a family tree
• 33 processes, from washed to hydro honey and thermal shock
• The coffee flavour wheel, coffee words, history and championships

YOUR BENCH, EVERYWHERE
• A profile with your nickname, photo and the coffee you love
• Sign in with email to sync your coffees, diary, brews and photos between your phones and the website
• Shared brew logs with friends
• Light and dark, eight coffee colour themes

No ads. No tracking. Everything works offline, and your data stays on your phone unless you choose to sign in.
