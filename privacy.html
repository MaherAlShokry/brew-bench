The Brew Bench

Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

The Brew Bench is a coffee field guide and dial-in tool, on the web at maheralshokry.github.io/brew-bench and as an Android app. It is made by Maher Al Shokry, who is responsible for your data under this policy. In short: what you save stays on your device unless you choose to sign in or share, there are no ads and no tracking, and you can delete everything at any time.

What stays on your device

Without an account, everything you save is kept only on your phone or in your browser: your coffees and their diaries, photos, brews, recipes, scans, gear, calibration, favourites and settings, and your profile (nickname, name, a few words about you, a photo or symbol, favourites and city). We never receive it.

Scanning a bag: the label is read on your device. The photo is not sent anywhere. The first time you scan, the app may download the text-reading software; that download sends no personal data.

Camera: used only when you choose to take a photo of a bag, a coffee or a diary entry.

Notifications (Android app, optional): the daily coffee reminder is scheduled on your phone.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. With an account, the following is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database, servers in the European Union) so it can follow you between your devices:

DataWhy
Email address, and a password or your Google account (name and profile photo if you sign in with Google)To create your account and sign you in
Your profileTo show it on all your devices
Your coffees, diaries, photos, brews, recipes, scans, Top 5, trophies, favourites, gear, calibration and app settingsTo sync them between your devices

Only you can read your account’s data: the database rules allow each account to read and write its own data and nothing else. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we don’t use it for advertising.

Shared brew logs

If you start or join a shared log, the brews and recipes you add to it, with your nickname, are stored in the same Firebase database and can be read and changed by anyone who has the log’s code or invite link. Share the code only with people you brew with. You can leave a shared log at any time.

Other services

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) stores accounts and synced data, as above. Firebase privacy information.
  • Google sign-in on the website, if you choose it.
  • The website loads its emoji font from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address as part of any web request.
  • Championship dates are read from the app’s own website. No personal data is sent.

There are no ads, analytics or tracking tools in the app or on the website.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Data on your device stays until you delete it or the app. Account data is kept while you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, synced data and photos are deleted from our database straight away, and your sign-in details are removed by Firebase Authentication. See how to delete your account.

You can also export everything you’ve saved as a file from Settings → Your data.

Your rights

You can see, correct, export or delete your data at any time from the app, or ask us to do it for you by emailing thehope99.ms@gmail.com. We’ll reply within 30 days.

Children

The Brew Bench is not directed at children under 13 and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
